02 Open the archive

Enter through
Obsidian.

The Mirrored Realms does not expect to be read from beginning to end. It expects curiosity. Open the repository as an Obsidian vault, start at the Home page, and follow whichever name first feels familiar.

Open source / Explore freely

The door was never locked.

Take what your table needs, change what the records got wrong, and follow the wiki-links until the vault begins answering questions you never asked.

Terminal
git clone https://github.com/ShadowOkami4/Mirrorgate.git
Download as ZIP ↓

  1. Clone or download

    Get the complete repository from GitHub.

  2. Open as a vault

    Open the folder in Obsidian 1.15 or newer and trust its community plugins when asked. The Mirrored Pages theme comes already set up.

  3. Find the entrance

    The Hearth start screen opens on its own. From there, Home.md leads to every section: Atlas, Characters, Factions, History, Rules and more.

  4. Written for the GM

    Every page tells the Game Master the whole truth; what players may know waits in a Player info box. Or read the same records as books in 10 Books.

04 Tools & workflow

The Scriptorium
behind the archive.

Every archive needs a scriptorium. The Mirrored Realms is written and connected in Obsidian, mapped in Canvas of Kings, and dressed in its own theme. TaleSpire waits for the stage when play sessions will need it.

Current workflow

Primary environment

Obsidian

The vault is the work itself. Every record is written, linked, and organised in Obsidian: the primary editor, the knowledge base, and the place where Kagameru is built.

Visit Obsidian ↗
Supporting plugin

Atlas VTT

A free, open-source Obsidian plugin by Fabian Urbanek (ByteMirror) that turns the vault into a virtual tabletop: battle maps, tokens, fog of war, dice, and initiative. Optional; every note works without it.

Atlas VTT on GitHub ↗
In use

Canvas of Kings

The maps of Kagameru are drawn here, from the world map down to the places the adventures lead to.

Canvas of Kings on Steam ↗
Own theme

Mirrored Pages

The vault’s own Obsidian theme: slate glass, brushed silver and statblocks for Atlas VTT. Made for this setting, free for every vault.

Planned workflow

Planned

TaleSpire

A planned 3D environment for building places of Kagameru as maps and bringing them to a virtual table. It is reserved for later-stage visualisation and play.

Visit TaleSpire ↗
Made by hand

The Mirrored Realms is made by Okami, about two years and many scrapped versions in the making. The story, its characters, and where it goes are written by hand. AI (Claude) only helps with checks: whether the map and the lore agree, grammar and spelling, some balancing of mechanics, and keeping the vault organised.
