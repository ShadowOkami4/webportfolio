01 Planned journeys

Eighteen doors.
No safe order.

Every planned adventure belongs to the same setting. A single night beside the wrong mirror may end at dawn, or return much later as the first thread of a history the players have already changed.

Standalone / 2 announced

10

One-shots

Ten doors into cursed estates, forbidden archives, unstable scars, and mirrors that promise to open only once.

Multishots / 3 announced

5

Short adventures

Five deeper trails where local conflicts expose recurring faces, dangerous relics, and consequences that refuse to remain where they began.

Long term / 1 announced

3

Campaign frameworks

Three long shadows over Zerkalo, where crowns, divine wounds, and the Mirrorgate can reshape an entire table’s version of the world. The first is Shattered Hope, the setting’s main campaign.

Current production

Two doors stand ajar.

Of the eighteen, only two are being written today. The other announced journeys have a public title and premise, but work on them has not begun; the remaining slots are reserved and still unnamed.

02 Beyond adventures

The stories are only
the first layer.

The Mirrored Realms is more than a collection of adventures. It is an interconnected worldbuilding archive, where lore, characters, places, rules, and adventures lead into one another.

In the vault

World & history

Zerkalo, its cosmology, lands, and politics, and the histories and myths that still disagree about the Primordial War.

In the vault

Peoples & powers

Species and cultures, faiths and deities, royal bloodlines, factions, and figures whose names appear more often than coincidence allows.

In progress

Rules & bestiary

Homebrew built on SRD 5.2.1: hazards for surviving the scars of the Primordial War, the shard-born Shardkin, and the first subclass drafts.

In progress

Adventures & fiction

The Pack in the Twilight and the Shattered Hope campaign are the first threads pulled from the dark.

In progress

The Bookshelf

The same records gathered into reading order: a setting guide, a Player’s Companion, a Game Master’s Codex, a bestiary, and one book per adventure.

Planned

Feats, spells & magic items

Further character options, spells, relics, and other objects whose histories may be more dangerous than their powers.

04 Tools & workflow

The Scriptorium
behind the archive.

Every archive needs a scriptorium. The Mirrored Realms is written and connected in Obsidian today; other tools wait for the stage of development when maps and play sessions will need them.

Current workflow

Primary environment

Obsidian

The vault is the work itself. Every record is written, linked, and organised in Obsidian: the primary editor, the knowledge base, and the place where Zerkalo is built.

Visit Obsidian ↗
Supporting plugin

Atlas VTT

An Obsidian plugin that brings a virtual tabletop into the vault. It supports the current tabletop workflow alongside the records, while Obsidian remains where they are written.

View the plugin ↗

Planned workflow

Planned

Canvas of Kings

The intended tool for the setting’s future maps. It belongs to a later stage of development and is not yet part of production.

Canvas of Kings on Steam ↗
Planned

TaleSpire

A planned 3D environment for building places of Zerkalo as maps and bringing them to a virtual table. It is reserved for later-stage visualisation and play.

Artwork note

AI-generated images in the vault are currently used as visual aids, concept references, and temporary representations during development. Original artist contributions are welcome; suitable work may replace them over time, and every accepted piece will credit its artist.
