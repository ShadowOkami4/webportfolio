02 Version 1 modules

Enable what matters.
Leave the rest quiet.

Every major feature is its own module. Turn on what your server needs.

Moderation & Safety

Warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, cleanup, moderation cases, AutoMod, and anti-spam.

Welcome & Onboarding

Welcome and goodbye messages, autoroles, verification, and onboarding flows.

Roles

Reaction, button, select, persistent, and self-service role menus.

Support & Tickets

Panels, categories, staff assignment, transcripts, claims, and ticket logs.

Levels & Rewards

XP, ranks, leaderboards, rewards, voice XP, and exclusions.

Voice Rooms

Join-to-create rooms, ownership, limits, locking, names, and permissions.

Logs

Moderation, member, message, channel, role, voice, and configuration events.

Music

Playback, queues, permissions, playlists, and voice controls.

Automation

Flexible trigger, condition, and action workflows.

Social Notifications

YouTube, Twitch, and potentially other service notifications.

Utilities

Server and user information, polls, reminders, and general Discord tools.

Built to expand

New modules can be added later without turning LunaEcho into separate bots.

Tickets

Module settings

Configuration stays yours.

Turning a module off stops it but keeps its settings.

03 Platform foundation

One codebase.
Three runtime modes.

Self-Hosted, Cloud and Custom are modes of one bot, not separate products.

SELF_HOSTEDCLOUD_SHAREDCLOUD_CUSTOM

Planned foundation

TypeScript throughout

One typed codebase for bot, API, dashboard and workers.

Node.jsdiscord.jsNext.jsPostgreSQLRedisDocker

Custom token security

A bot token is a password.

Encrypted at rest, never logged, never shown again.

Two permission layers

Discord access is not billing access.

Discord roles decide what the bot may do; dashboard roles decide what staff may change.

Support boundaries

Responsibility follows hosting.

Self-Hosted: docs and community. Cloud: best effort. Custom: managed support.
