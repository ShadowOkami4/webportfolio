02 Feature access

Useful free.
Powerful when needed.

Lightweight everyday tools stay in the managed Free tier. Features that continuously use audio, storage, or background processing are limited or moved to Premium and Self-hosted.

01

Moderation

Core safety tools remain available to every server.

FreeIncluded

Warnings, timeouts, kick, ban, and message cleanup.

PremiumFull suite

Rule presets, repeat-offender actions, and deeper case tools.

Self-hostedFull suite

All moderation features without managed quotas.

02

Onboarding & roles

Simple welcomes are free; larger flows unlock with the full tiers.

FreeIncluded

One welcome setup and one automatic role.

PremiumFull suite

Multiple flows, role menus, templates, and conditions.

Self-hostedFull suite

Complete onboarding configuration on your server.

03

Ticket system

Small communities get a practical starting point without storage growing unchecked.

FreeLimited

1 panel, up to 3 open tickets, and 7-day transcripts.

PremiumFull suite

Self-hostedFull suite

No managed limit; storage depends on your host.

04

The social core stays free while deeper customization belongs to the full tiers.

FreeIncluded

Free includes full room usage with a simple limit on creator hubs.

Server logging

Essential records are free; broader coverage and retention use more storage.

FreeLimited

Self-hostedFull suite

07

Music

Continuous audio is one of the most resource-heavy managed features.

FreeNot included

Music is excluded to keep the managed Free tier sustainable.

PremiumIncluded

Managed queue playback and complete member controls.

Self-hostedIncluded

Music runs using your own infrastructure.

08

Automations & insights

Background workflows and stored activity analysis are reserved for full access.

FreeNot included

Advanced rules, schedules, and analytics are unavailable.

PremiumIncluded

Advanced rules, scheduled actions, and community insights.

Self-hostedIncluded

All workflows and insights run on your hardware.

Why limits?

Free should be useful, not overloaded.

Everyday moderation, onboarding, and community basics remain free. Continuous audio, long-term storage, and background processing create ongoing hosting costs, so those features are limited or reserved for Premium and Self-hosted.
