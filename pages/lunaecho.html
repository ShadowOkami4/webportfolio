02 Feature scope

A useful core.
A complete path forward.

Managed Free covers practical everyday tools. Managed Complete adds resource-intensive and advanced features, while Self-Hosted provides the complete scope on infrastructure you operate.

01

Moderation

Core safety tools remain available to every server.

Managed FreeIncluded

Warnings, timeouts, kick, ban, and message cleanup.

Managed CompleteFull suite

Rule presets, repeat-offender actions, and deeper case tools.

Self-HostedFull suite

All moderation features without managed quotas.

02

Onboarding & roles

Managed Free covers simple welcomes; full-access models unlock larger flows.

Managed FreeIncluded

One welcome setup and one automatic role.

Managed CompleteFull suite

Multiple flows, role menus, templates, and conditions.

Self-HostedFull suite

Complete onboarding configuration on your server.

03

Ticket system

Small communities get a practical starting point without storage growing unchecked.

Managed FreeLimited

1 panel, up to 3 open tickets, and 7-day transcripts.

Managed CompleteFull suite

Multiple panels, higher limits, routing, and longer transcripts.

Self-HostedFull suite

No managed limit; storage depends on your host.

04

Leveling & rewards

The social core stays in Managed Free while deeper customization belongs to full access.

Managed FreeIncluded

XP, ranks, and a basic server leaderboard.

Managed CompleteFull suite

Role rewards, multipliers, exclusions, and seasons.

Self-HostedFull suite

All progression and reward controls included.

05

Temporary voice

Managed Free includes full room usage with a simple limit on creator hubs.

Managed Free2 hubs

Up to 2 creator hubs with no limit on active temporary rooms.

Managed CompleteFull suite

More creator hubs plus custom names and member permissions.

Self-HostedFull suite

No LunaEcho-managed room limits.

06

Server logging

Essential records are included in Managed Free; broader coverage and retention use more storage.

Managed FreeLimited

Essential moderation and member logs kept for 7 days.

Managed CompleteFull suite

More event types, longer retention, and searchable summaries.

Self-HostedFull suite

You choose the coverage and available storage.

07

Music

Continuous audio is one of the most resource-heavy managed features.

Managed FreeNot included

Music is excluded to keep Managed Free sustainable.

Managed CompleteIncluded

Managed queue playback and complete member controls.

Self-HostedIncluded

Music runs using your own infrastructure.

08

Automations & insights

Background workflows and stored activity analysis are reserved for full access.

Managed FreeNot included

Advanced rules, schedules, and analytics are unavailable.

Managed CompleteIncluded

Advanced rules, scheduled actions, and community insights.

Self-HostedIncluded

All workflows and insights run on your hardware.

Why limits?

Managed Free should be useful, not overloaded.

Everyday moderation, onboarding, and community basics remain available at no cost. Continuous audio, long-term storage, and background processing create ongoing hosting costs, so those features move to Managed Complete or Self-Hosted.

03 Release roadmap

Built in stages.
Released when ready.

The roadmap describes the intended release sequence rather than promising dates. Reliability, documentation, and operational readiness come before opening LunaEcho to public servers.

01 / NowIn development

Core platform

Build and connect the moderation, community, ticket, voice, music, and automation foundations.

  • Core feature architecture
  • Permissions and configuration model
  • Reliable Discord event handling
02 / NextPlanned

Controlled testing

Validate real server workflows before opening the project more widely.

  • Feature and limit validation
  • Reliability and recovery testing
  • Setup and operator documentation
03 / ThenPlanned

Self-Hosted preview

Publish the repository when the self-hosted experience is understandable and supportable.

  • Public source repository
  • Installation and update guidance
  • Initial configuration reference
04 / LaterPlanned

Managed launch

Introduce Managed Free and Managed Complete after hosting operations are ready.

  • Managed onboarding flow
  • Fair-use limits and monitoring
  • Clear upgrade and billing information

No release date yet: Milestones may change as LunaEcho is built and tested. Public availability will only be announced when the relevant stage is ready.
