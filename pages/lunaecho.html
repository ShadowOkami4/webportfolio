Custom pricing

Pay for your bot and its size. Nothing else.

  1. €3 per botCovers what every bot costs: its own container and its own database.
  2. Members in steps of 1,000The first 1,000 are free. Each step's rate applies only to the members inside it, like tax brackets, so crossing an edge never makes the price jump.
  3. 30-day averageEach bot's servers are counted once a day and the 30-day average counts, so a raid or a busy week barely changes anything.
  4. Size changes at renewalA new size applies from your next monthly bill, never mid-month. When your community shrinks, the price follows automatically.
  5. More or fewer bots any timeChange the number on the dashboard. A new bot is charged for the rest of the month; a removed one leaves a credit for your next bill.

02 Modules

Enable what matters.
Leave the rest quiet.

All eleven are built. Each is its own module; turn on what your server needs.

Moderation & Safety

Warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, purges, and case history. AutoMod with four profiles, ten rules, and a banned-word filter.

Welcome & Onboarding

Welcome and goodbye messages with picture cards, a welcome DM, autoroles, and a verification button.

Roles

Self-assignable role menus with buttons or a drop-down.

Support & Tickets

Private ticket threads with claim, close, and transcripts.

Levels & Rewards

Message and voice XP, role rewards, /rank, and a leaderboard.

Voice Rooms

Join-to-create rooms. Owners rename, limit, lock, hide, and hand them over with /room.

Logs

Messages, members, server changes, voice, and moderation cases, each to its own channel.

Music

Play, queue, loop, shuffle, and volume through a Lavalink audio node, with an optional DJ role.

Custom & Self-Hosted

Automation

Rules with keyword, join, or schedule triggers, conditions, and actions.
