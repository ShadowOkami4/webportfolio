01 Features

One bot.
A lot of ground.

LunaEcho is planned as a broad server platform, not a small command bundle. Each system is designed to work together while still being configurable on its own.

MD

Moderation

Everyday actions, case history, rule presets, and automated responses for safer servers.

ON

Onboarding & roles

Welcome flows, auto-roles, role menus, and configurable paths for new members.

MU

Music

Queue-based playback, shared controls, and reliable audio for active voice channels.

VC

Temporary voice

Voice rooms created on demand with custom names, limits, and member permissions.

TK

Ticket system

Structured support requests with categories, staff routing, permissions, and transcripts.

XP

Leveling & rewards

Experience, leaderboards, role rewards, multipliers, and seasonal progression.

LG

Server logging

Useful records for moderation, members, roles, messages, and important server changes.

AU

Automations

Scheduled actions and configurable rules that handle repetitive community work.

IN

Community insights

Activity summaries and practical trends without turning the server into a spreadsheet.

03 Feature access

Useful free.
Powerful when needed.

Lightweight everyday tools stay in the managed Free tier. Features that continuously use audio, storage, or background processing are limited or moved to Premium and Self-hosted.

Why limits?

Free should be useful, not overloaded.

Everyday moderation, onboarding, and community basics remain free. Continuous audio, long-term storage, and background processing create ongoing hosting costs, so those features are limited or reserved for Premium and Self-hosted.
