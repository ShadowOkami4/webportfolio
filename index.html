01 About

No studio. No grand plan.
Just ideas worth following.

I’m Okami, a hobby developer still early in the journey. I learn by making things I genuinely want to use, from Linux desktop ideas and automation to Discord tools and tabletop worlds.

Personal use comes first. If a project becomes useful, interesting, or fun to share, I make it public so others can explore or adapt it. YouTube mixes gaming with project previews and demos; Twitch is where I stream for fun, separate from my coding projects.

THE SIMPLE PROCESS

An idea catches my attention. I build the version I would want to use, learn what the project demands, and share it once there is something worth exploring.

AI TRANSPARENCY

AI is part of the toolbox.

I use AI tools as assistants for brainstorming, prototype generation, troubleshooting, and learning. I review and adapt the results, make the final project decisions, and remain responsible for everything I publish.
